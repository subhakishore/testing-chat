FID-10: No Connection to Unit
The recovery steps depend on the module affected. Here are the details:
ABIL (FSP Subunit Lost / No Connection to Unit)
Applicable SW: 25R3, 26R1, 26R2
1. Wait for automatic recovery resets (3 times)
2. If ABIL not recovered after auto recovery:
o Perform BBMOD power off/power on (with 1-hour waiting period)
o Perform Web-EM site power reset
AEHC (No Connection to Unit)
Applicable SW: 25R3, 26R1, 26R2 | Waiting Period: 120 Minutes
1. Collect Pre and Post Snapshot
2. Engage R&D ASAP (No recovery before engaging R&D)
3. Wait up to 2 hours for self-recovery (No TSH required within waiting period)
4. Check if RU unit is reachable/pinging, then collect RU logs
AHLOB / AHFII (No Connection to Unit)
Applicable SW: 25R3, 26R1, 26R2 | Waiting Period: 45 Minutes
1. Collect Pre and Post Snapshot
2. Check Rx power at BB from the unit
3. Try SFP procedure and wait minimum 20 minutes
4. The unit should recover on its own in most cases
AHFII, AHLOA, AHLOB, AHFIG (FID-10 at NR End)
• LTE module is Up and Running
• Perform pssreboot HW from LTE end using SSH
AHFII, AHLOA, AHLOB, AHFIG (FID-10 at LTE End)
• NR module is Up and Running
• Perform Web-EM RMOD reset from NR
NOTE: During troubleshooting, if FID-10 turns into FID-1850 / FID-23 / FID-2934 / FID-
2935 / FID-2942, it means connectivity to RU is established. Collect RU logs and snapshot
at this point for R&D investigation.
